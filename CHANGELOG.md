# Changelog

## Unreleased

- The selected chat channel is remembered when you switch servers (for example `/hub`), reconnect or restart the
  game, like Hypixel does. Before, the buttons went back to Normal while Hypixel still sent messages to the old channel.

## 1.0.0

First release, for Minecraft 26.2 and 26.3 (Fabric).

- Channel buttons above the chat input: Normal, Party, Guild and Co-op, plus your own command buttons.
  The active channel is highlighted and follows Hypixel's channel messages.
- Player heads in guild, party, co-op, private message, Party Finder and SkyBlock chat, also for players who are
  not in your lobby.
- Chat history of 1000 messages (100–10000), longer sent message history, optional "keep chat on disconnect".
- Chat peek: hold Left Alt to see the whole chat without opening it; the mouse wheel scrolls it.
- Settings screen with `/chatplus`, a ⚙ button in chat and Mod Menu support.
- English and Turkish translations.
